\chapter*{ABSTRACT}

\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

The Summer Internship was carried out at \textbf{Cognifyz IT Solutions Pvt. Ltd.} with the objective of gaining practical exposure to Data Science methodologies and applying analytical techniques to a real-world business problem. The internship focused on analysing a restaurant dataset containing information related to restaurant locations, cuisines, pricing, customer ratings, votes, online delivery services, and table booking facilities.

The primary objective of the project was to transform raw restaurant data into meaningful business insights through a structured Data Science workflow. The work included dataset understanding, data cleaning, preprocessing, exploratory data analysis, descriptive statistical analysis, geospatial analysis, feature engineering, customer preference analysis, predictive modelling, and data visualization. Python programming language was used throughout the internship along with widely adopted Data Science libraries such as Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Plotly, Folium, and Scikit-learn.

During the internship, several business factors influencing restaurant performance were identified. The analysis demonstrated that customer ratings are affected by multiple variables including cuisine type, geographical location, price range, online delivery availability, and customer engagement. Data preprocessing significantly improved the quality of the dataset, while exploratory analysis helped reveal hidden relationships among various restaurant attributes. Machine learning models were further developed to predict restaurant ratings based on selected features.

The internship provided practical experience in the complete Data Science lifecycle, beginning with raw data acquisition and ending with predictive modelling and business insight generation. It strengthened technical knowledge in Python programming, statistical analysis, machine learning, and visualization while improving analytical thinking and problem-solving abilities. The project successfully demonstrated how Data Science techniques can support evidence-based decision-making in the restaurant industry through systematic analysis of real-world data.

\chapter{PROBLEM STATEMENT}

This chapter explains the business problem addressed during the internship. It discusses the background of the restaurant industry, the challenges associated with analysing restaurant datasets, the factors contributing to these challenges, and the need for applying Data Science techniques to transform raw data into meaningful business insights.

%-----------------------------------------------------------------------

\section{Background}

The rapid growth of online food delivery platforms, restaurant discovery applications, and digital customer feedback systems has significantly increased the amount of information generated within the restaurant industry. Every customer interaction produces valuable data such as restaurant ratings, customer votes, cuisine preferences, pricing information, geographical location, online delivery availability, and table booking facilities.

Although this information has tremendous business value, much of it remains underutilized because of its volume, diversity, and complexity. Restaurant owners often rely on intuition and limited observations instead of systematic data analysis while making business decisions. Consequently, opportunities to improve customer satisfaction, pricing strategies, and operational efficiency may remain unidentified.

Data Science provides an effective framework for converting such raw business data into meaningful knowledge that supports informed decision-making.

%-----------------------------------------------------------------------

\section{Problem Description}

The Restaurant Data Analysis project was designed to analyse a real-world dataset containing thousands of restaurant records collected from multiple cities and countries. Each record contains numerous attributes describing restaurant characteristics, customer behaviour, and service availability.

The primary challenge lies in understanding how different variables collectively influence restaurant performance. Customer ratings are affected by multiple factors including cuisine type, geographical location, pricing strategy, customer engagement, online delivery services, and table booking facilities. Analysing these relationships manually is difficult because of the size and complexity of the dataset.

Without systematic analysis, businesses may fail to identify the factors responsible for customer satisfaction and overall restaurant success.

%-----------------------------------------------------------------------

\section{Reasons Behind the Problem}

Several practical factors contribute to the complexity of restaurant data analysis.

\subsection{Large Volume of Data}

Restaurant datasets typically contain thousands of observations and multiple descriptive variables. Manual examination of such datasets is inefficient and increases the possibility of overlooking important relationships among variables.

\subsection{Poor Data Quality}

Real-world datasets frequently contain missing values, duplicate records, inconsistent formatting, incorrect data types, and incomplete information. Such issues reduce the reliability of statistical analysis unless appropriate preprocessing techniques are applied.

\subsection{Multiple Influencing Variables}

Restaurant performance depends on numerous interconnected business factors rather than a single variable. Customer satisfaction is influenced simultaneously by food quality, pricing, location, customer service, cuisine type, and online services, making interpretation significantly more complex.

\section{Causes of the Problem}

The major causes responsible for inefficient restaurant decision-making include:

\begin{itemize}

\item Lack of systematic data analysis.

\item Poor quality datasets.

\item Manual interpretation of business information.

\item Limited use of visualization techniques.

\item Difficulty in identifying hidden relationships among multiple variables.

\item Absence of predictive analytical models for supporting business decisions.

\end{itemize}

These challenges demonstrate the necessity of adopting Data Science methodologies capable of transforming raw restaurant data into actionable business knowledge.

\section{Dataset Understanding}

The first stage involved understanding the structure and characteristics of the restaurant dataset before performing any preprocessing or modelling activities.

\subsection{Dataset Overview}

The dataset consisted of restaurant information collected from multiple countries and cities. Each record represented an individual restaurant and contained attributes describing restaurant characteristics, pricing, cuisines, geographical coordinates, customer ratings, online delivery, table booking availability, and customer votes.

\subsection{Data Inspection}

Initial inspection included examination of:

\begin{itemize}
\item Dataset dimensions.
\item Feature names.
\item Data types.
\item Missing values.
\item Duplicate records.
\item Summary statistics.
\item Target variable distribution.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Observations}

The exploration stage revealed that the dataset contained a combination of numerical and categorical variables. Several attributes required preprocessing before further analysis. Missing values and duplicate records were identified and addressed in the following phase.

%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\section{Data Cleaning}

Real-world datasets often contain inconsistencies that reduce analytical reliability. Therefore, preprocessing was performed before statistical analysis.

\subsection{Handling Missing Values}

Missing values were identified using Python data analysis libraries. Appropriate treatments were applied depending on the nature of each variable. Numerical variables were validated while categorical variables were inspected for incomplete information.

\subsection{Duplicate Removal}

Duplicate restaurant records were examined and removed where necessary to prevent bias during statistical analysis and predictive modelling.

\subsection{Data Type Validation}

Each feature was verified to ensure that numerical attributes were stored using appropriate numeric formats while categorical variables retained meaningful labels.

\subsection{Validation}

After preprocessing, the dataset was re-evaluated to confirm the absence of significant inconsistencies. This improved the reliability of subsequent analytical stages.

Descriptive analysis was performed to summarize restaurant characteristics and customer behaviour.

\subsection{Numerical Analysis}

\subsection{Categorical Analysis}

Categorical variables such as country, city, cuisine type, price range, online delivery availability, and table booking were analysed using frequency distributions.

\subsection{Business Insights}

The descriptive analysis highlighted several important observations.

\begin{itemize}
\item Restaurant ratings varied across countries and cities.
\item Customer voting behaviour differed significantly among restaurants.
\item Certain cuisines appeared much more frequently than others.
\item Online delivery and table booking facilities were concentrated within specific restaurant categories.
\item Pricing levels influenced customer preferences and restaurant popularity.
\end{itemize}

The findings obtained during descriptive analysis established the foundation for subsequent geospatial analysis, feature engineering, predictive modelling, and customer preference analysis.
