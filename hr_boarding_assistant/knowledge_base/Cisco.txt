SECTION 1: EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING STEPS
----------------------------

Step 1: Pre-Joining Formalities
- Accept offer letter via Cisco's e-onboarding portal (Workday / Cisco HR Portal).
- Complete I-9 verification (US employees) or equivalent ID verification (international).
- Submit required documents: government ID, tax forms (W-4 for US / equivalent), bank details for direct deposit, emergency contact information.
- Background check initiated through Cisco's third-party verification vendor (Sterling).
- Receive IT setup instructions and pre-joining resources via email.

Step 2: Pre-Boarding (Week Before Joining)
- Receive Cisco laptop and equipment shipped to your home address (remote hires) or available at office.
- IT account activation email sent; set up Cisco SSO, Duo Security (MFA), WebEx, and Jabber.
- Complete required pre-joining compliance modules (available online before Day 1).
- Introduction email from hiring manager with team calendar and Day 1 schedule.

Step 3: Day 1 – Welcome & Orientation
- Join Global New Employee Orientation (virtual or in-person depending on location).
- Introduction to Cisco's purpose: "To continuously advance and connect the unconnected."
- Overview of Cisco's values: Dedicated to your success, Give back, Innovation, Trust, and Integrity.
- Cisco People Deal introduced: "Be a part of something powerful, pursue better together, be brilliant."
- Complete Day 1 compliance acknowledgments in Workday.

Step 4: First 30 Days – Onboarding Plan
- Complete the 30-60-90 Day Onboarding Plan assigned by manager in Workday.
- Introduction to key stakeholders, team processes, and internal tools (Cisco dCloud, Webex Teams, Cisco DevNet).
- Attend department-specific onboarding sessions.
- Enroll in Cisco Learning & Certifications portal (NetAcad, Cisco Learning Network).
- Set initial goals in Workday with manager.

Step 5: First 60–90 Days
- Deep-dive into product, technical, or business domain training.
- Participate in New Employee Experience (NEX) programs and networking events.
- Regular 1:1 meetings with manager and skip-level check-in.
- Mid-onboarding feedback session with HR.

Step 6: Probation & Confirmation
- Probation: Typically 6 months (varies by country/employment type).
- Performance discussed throughout; formal check-in at 6 months.

SECTION 2: HR POLICIES
----------------------------

2.1 Code of Business Conduct (COBC)
- All employees must complete the Code of Business Conduct training annually.
- Covers anti-bribery, anti-corruption, fair competition, conflicts of interest, insider trading, and export controls.
- Violations reported via Cisco Integrity Line (anonymous, 24/7, available in 150+ countries).
- Non-compliance may lead to disciplinary action up to and including termination.

2.2 Diversity, Equity & Inclusion (DEI) Policy
- Cisco is committed to a workforce that reflects the diversity of its customers and communities.
- Equal Opportunity Employer: hiring decisions based solely on qualifications.
- Employees encouraged to join Cisco's Inclusion Communities (e.g., Cisco Connected Women, PRIDE, Black Cisco Employees).
- Discrimination or bias based on race, gender, nationality, age, disability, sexual orientation, or religion is not tolerated.

2.3 Global Anti-Harassment Policy
- Zero-tolerance policy for workplace harassment, bullying, and discrimination.
- Reporting channels: Direct manager, HR Business Partner, Cisco Integrity Line.
- All complaints investigated promptly and confidentially.

2.4 Information Security Policy
- All employees must complete annual information security and data privacy training.
- Cisco data (client, financial, product) classified and handled per Cisco's Information Security Policy.
- Clean desk policy in Cisco offices. No tailgating through secure access points.
- Use of non-approved personal devices for Cisco work is prohibited without MDM enrollment.

2.5 Responsible Internet Use Policy
- Cisco systems are for business use. Incidental personal use is permitted within boundaries.
- Accessing inappropriate, offensive, or illegal content on Cisco networks is prohibited.
- All internet activity on Cisco networks may be monitored.

2.6 Sustainability & Social Responsibility
- Employees are encouraged to participate in Cisco's CSR initiatives: Time2Give (5 days of paid volunteer time per year).
- Carbon footprint reduction and environmental sustainability are core to Cisco culture.

SECTION 3: LEAVE POLICY
----------------------------

Paid Time Off (PTO) – US Employees:
- Cisco follows a Flexible PTO policy for most employees in the US: no fixed annual PTO cap.
- Employees are encouraged to take adequate time off in coordination with their manager.
- Minimum 10 days off per year encouraged by Cisco leadership.
- Unused PTO does not carry over or get paid out under flexible PTO policy.

Sick Leave (US & Global):
- Separate sick leave entitlements apply as per local/state law requirements.
- Cisco provides paid sick leave that complies with or exceeds local requirements.
- Medical certification may be required for extended illness (5+ consecutive days).

Global Leave (Non-US Employees):
- Leave entitlements follow local labor laws and statutory requirements.
- India employees: PL – 21 days/year; SL – 15 days/year; CL – 8 days/year (subject to local policy).

Maternity Leave:
- US: Up to 12 weeks fully paid parental leave (birth mother) + STD (Short-Term Disability) benefits for physical recovery.
- India & Global: As per local Maternity Benefit laws. Cisco generally provides enhanced benefits beyond statutory requirements.

Paternity / Partner Leave:
- US: Up to 6 weeks fully paid parental leave for the non-birthing parent.
- India: 5 days (statutory) with additional optional leave available.

Adoption Leave:
- Up to 6 weeks paid leave for primary caregiver (US); statutory compliance globally.

Bereavement Leave:
- US: Up to 10 days for immediate family; 3 days for extended family.
- India: 5 days for immediate family.

Jury Duty / Civic Leave:
- Employees are granted paid time off for jury duty with proof of summons.

Volunteer Time Off (Time2Give):
- Up to 5 paid days per year for approved volunteer activities.

Leave Application Process:
- Apply via Workday HR portal under "Time Off."
- Manager approval required for all planned leaves.

SECTION 4: ATTENDANCE POLICY
----------------------------

Working Hours:
- Standard US: 40 hours/week, flexible scheduling.
- India/APAC: 9 hours/day (including 30-min break), 5 days/week.
- Core hours vary by team; typically 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM local time.

Flexible Work Arrangements:
- Cisco offers flexible work models: in-office, hybrid, and remote.
- Remote-first or hybrid designation discussed at time of hiring.
- Employees working remotely are accountable for availability and deliverables.

Hybrid/Remote Policy:
- Cisco supports a hybrid work model (Hybrid Work Index actively promoted by Cisco).
- Office days coordinated by managers for team collaboration.
- No minimum mandatory in-office days unless role-specific (e.g., lab/hardware roles).

Overtime:
- Non-exempt employees: Overtime approved by manager; paid per local law (1.5x for US hourly workers).
- Exempt employees: Overtime not separately compensated; comp-off at manager's discretion.

Attendance Tracking:
- Time and attendance tracked via Workday.
- Badge access systems in Cisco offices.
- Remote employees tracked by VPN/productivity metrics at manager discretion.

SECTION 6: EXIT / SEPARATION POLICY
----------------------------
