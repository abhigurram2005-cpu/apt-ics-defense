SECTION 1: EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING STEPS
----------------------------

Step 1: Pre-Joining Formalities
- Accept offer letter via Accenture's onboarding portal (myJoining).
- Submit required documents: educational certificates, ID proof, address proof, relieving letters, PAN card, passport-size photos.
- Complete background verification (BGV) through HireRight (third-party vendor).
- Receive joining instructions, date, and location via HR email.

Step 2: Day 1 – Joining Day
- Report to designated office or join virtual onboarding session.
- Collect/activate employee ID badge.
- IT provisioning: laptop, VPN credentials, Accenture email ID (@accenture.com).
- Introduction to assigned buddy/mentor.

Step 3: New Joiner Orientation (NJO)
- Attend mandatory New Joiner Orientation (in-person or virtual).
- Overview of Accenture's core values: Stewardship, Best People, Client Value Creation, One Global Network, Respect for the Individual, Integrity.
- Introduction to internal tools: Workday, myScheduling, Microsoft Teams, SharePoint.
- Complete Ethics & Compliance training within first week (mandatory).

Step 4: Role-Specific Onboarding
- Meeting with direct manager for role expectations and KPIs.
- Enroll in Technology Quotient (TQ) or domain-specific training via myLearning.
- Access granted to project tools and systems.

Step 5: Probation Period
- Probation: 6 months from date of joining.
- Performance check-in at 3 months; final review at 6 months.
- Employment confirmed after successful probation.

Step 6: Payroll & Benefits Enrollment
- Enroll in payroll via Workday within the first week.
- Select salary components (HRA, special allowance) via Compensation Planner.
- Enroll in health insurance and benefits via the benefits portal.

SECTION 2: HR POLICIES
----------------------------

2.1 Code of Business Ethics (COBE)
- All employees must read and acknowledge COBE annually.
- Zero tolerance for bribery, corruption, and conflict of interest.
- Report violations via Accenture Ethics Hotline (anonymous reporting available).
- Gifts above INR 5,000 / USD 75 must be declined or reported.

2.2 Workplace Harassment Policy
- Zero tolerance for harassment (sexual, verbal, physical).
- Raise concerns via HR Business Partner, Ethics Hotline, or Speak Up platform.
- Investigations completed within 30 days of complaint filing.
- Retaliation against complainants is strictly prohibited.

2.3 Data Privacy & Confidentiality
- No sharing of client or company data with unauthorized personnel.
- Compliance with GDPR and local data protection laws is mandatory.
- NDA signed at joining; violation is a terminable offense.

2.4 Social Media Policy
- Do not disclose client names, project details, or confidential information on social media.
- Use of Accenture branding in personal posts requires prior approval.

2.5 Dress Code Policy
- Business casual is the standard; formal attire for client-facing roles.
- Smart casuals permitted on Fridays.

SECTION 3: LEAVE POLICY
----------------------------

Annual/Privilege Leave (PL):
- 22 days/year (less than 5 years tenure); 24 days/year (5+ years tenure).
- Accrued monthly (~1.83 days/month). Max carry-forward: 30 days.
- Encashment allowed at resignation/retirement (up to 60 days).

Sick Leave (SL):
- 12 days/year. Medical certificate required for absences exceeding 2 consecutive days.
- Cannot be carried forward or encashed.

Casual Leave (CL):
- 8 days/year. Cannot be combined with other leave types or carried forward.

Maternity Leave:
- 26 weeks fully paid (as per Maternity Benefit Amendment Act, 2017 – India).
- Additional 12 weeks unpaid leave available on request.

Paternity Leave:
- 5 days paid leave within 3 months of child's birth/adoption.

Bereavement Leave:
- 5 days for immediate family; 3 days for extended family.

Study/Exam Leave:
- Up to 10 days/year for approved certifications. Manager pre-approval required.

Leave Application Process:
- Apply via Workday (minimum 3 days advance notice for planned leave).
- Emergency leave can be applied same day via Workday or email to manager.

SECTION 4: ATTENDANCE POLICY
----------------------------

Working Hours:
- 9 hours/day (including 1-hour break), Monday–Friday.
- Core hours: 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM. Flexible start: 7:30 AM – 10:00 AM.
- Total weekly hours: 40–45 hours.

Attendance Tracking:
- Biometric (in-office) or VPN login (remote).
- 3+ late arrivals per month triggers an HR notification.

Work From Home (WFH):
- Hybrid model: minimum 3 days/week in office (project-dependent).
- WFH requests submitted via Workday; manager approval required.
- Remote employees must be reachable during core hours.

Comp-Off / Overtime:
- Overtime compensated via comp-off (must be availed within 30 days).

AWOL (Absence Without Leave):
- 2+ consecutive unnotified absence days = AWOL; may result in disciplinary action or termination.

SECTION 5: BENEFITS & COMPENSATION
----------------------------

Health & Insurance:
- Group Medical Insurance (GMC): Up to INR 5,00,000/year (employee + spouse + 2 children).
- Group Term Life Insurance: 3x annual CTC.
- Group Personal Accident Insurance: 5x annual CTC.
- Annual health check-up for employees above 35 years.

Retirement:
- Provident Fund (PF): 12% employer contribution on basic salary.
- Gratuity: Eligible after 5 years of continuous service.
- National Pension Scheme (NPS): Optional enrollment with employer contribution.

Learning & Development:
- Access to myLearning (200,000+ courses).
- Certification reimbursement: Up to INR 25,000 / USD 500 per year.
- Sponsored MBA programs for high performers.

Allowances:
- HRA as per city classification. Transport Allowance: INR 1,600/month.
- Mobile/Internet Reimbursement: Up to INR 1,500/month (eligible roles).

Wellness:
- Employee Assistance Program (EAP): 8 free counseling sessions/year.
- Gym reimbursement: Up to INR 5,000/year.

Performance & Rewards:
- Annual review cycle. Ratings: Exceptional, High, Successful, Needs Improvement.
- Merit increments in July. Spot awards and Team Achievement Awards available.

----------------------------
SECTION 6: EXIT / SEPARATION POLICY
----------------------------

- Notice Period: 60–90 days (as per grade/appointment letter).
- Submit resignation via Workday. Exit interview is mandatory.
- Return all assets (laptop, ID badge, access cards) on last working day.
- Full & Final settlement within 30–45 days post last working day.
- Relieving and experience letters issued within 15 days post exit.
