SECTION 1: EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING STEPS ("Noogler" Experience)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

NOTE: New Google employees are called "Nooglers" (New + Googler).

STEP 1 – PRE-JOINING
----------------------
- Accept offer through Google's hiring portal (Workday).
- Complete electronic background check consent (Checkr or equivalent).
- Upload documents: government-issued ID, academic credentials, prior employment records.
- Receive "Pre-Noogler" welcome email with Day 1 preparation guide.
- Google account provisioned 24–48 hours before Day 1.
- Access Google's Pre-Onboarding Portal: "Arriving at Google" resource page.
- Review the Google Code of Conduct ("Don't Be Evil" ethos; updated to "Do the Right Thing").

STEP 2 – DAY 1: NOOGLER ORIENTATION
--------------------------------------
- Receive iconic "Noogler" propeller beanie hat on Day 1 (a Google tradition).
- Report to designated Google campus or join virtual onboarding (Googleplex or India campus).
- Attend the "TGIF-style" Welcome Session (or virtual equivalent for remote hires).
- Receive Google Chromebook/MacBook, Google Badge, and all required equipment.
- Complete IT setup: Google Workspace (Gmail, Docs, Meet, Drive, Chat), Titan MFA device.
- Meet with manager and team.
- Campus/office tour and facilities walkthrough (on-site).

STEP 3 – WEEK 1: FOUNDATIONS
------------------------------
- Complete mandatory compliance and code of conduct courses in Google's LMS.
  Required modules:
  - Google Code of Conduct
  - Information Security & Privacy
  - Insider Trading Policy (for eligible roles)
  - Respectful Workplace training
- Attend functional orientation sessions for your specific role (Engineering, Sales, Ops, etc.).
- Set up 1:1 cadence with manager.
- Join Google internal groups: Memegen, Dory, and team Spaces on Chat.
- Explore Google's internal wiki: "g3docs" and "go/noogler".

STEP 4 – FIRST 30 DAYS
------------------------
- Enroll in health and wellness benefits within 30 days.
- Submit payroll and tax documentation via Workday (India: Form 12BB, bank details).
- Shadow team meetings, product reviews, and sprint planning sessions.
- Get access to Google's internal code repositories (for engineering roles).
- Set OKRs (Objectives and Key Results) for the quarter with manager.
- Attend Google Noogler Networking events.

STEP 5 – 90-DAY CHECK-IN
---------------------------
- Manager-led 90-day onboarding review.
- Assess integration, culture fit, and initial performance.
- Probation confirmation (for applicable geographies including India).
- Finalize Individual Career Development Plan with manager.

SECTION 2: LEAVE POLICY (INDIA)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

PAID TIME OFF (PTO)
--------------------
- Google India follows a structured leave policy aligned with local labor laws.
- Annual Earned/Privilege Leave: 24 days per year (2 per month accrual).
- Carry forward: Up to 30 days.
- Encashable on separation.
- Required advance notice: 5 working days for planned leaves.

SICK LEAVE
-----------
- 12 days per year.
- Non-encashable; no carry forward.
- Doctor's certificate required for 3+ consecutive sick days.

CASUAL LEAVE
-------------
- 8 days per year.
- No carry forward; no encashment.

MATERNITY LEAVE
----------------
- 26 weeks for first and second child.
- Google India enhanced maternity benefit: Full pay for statutory duration.
- Flexible return-to-work: Option for gradual ramp-up.
- New parent coaching and peer parent support available.

PATERNITY LEAVE
----------------
- Google provides up to 18 weeks paid paternity leave (significantly above statutory).
- Taken anytime within 12 months of birth, adoption, or surrogacy.

PARENTAL LEAVE (PRIMARY/SECONDARY CAREGIVER)
----------------------------------------------
- Primary caregiver (regardless of gender): 18 weeks paid.
- Secondary caregiver: 6 weeks paid.
- Covers birth, adoption, foster care, and surrogacy.

BEREAVEMENT LEAVE
------------------
- Immediate family (parent, spouse, child, sibling): Up to 5 days.
- Extended family: 3 days.
- Pregnancy/infant loss leave: Up to 20 days (Google Compassionate Care Policy).

MARRIAGE LEAVE
---------------
- 5 working days for own wedding.

CAREGIVER LEAVE
----------------
- Up to 4 weeks per year to care for a seriously ill family member.

MENTAL HEALTH / WELLNESS LEAVE
--------------------------------
- Google recognizes mental health leave under sick leave.
- "Care Days": Additional discretionary days at manager's approval for personal wellness.

LEAVE APPLICATION PROCESS
---------------------------
- All leaves applied through Workday.
- Manager approval required.
- Integrated with Google Calendar for team visibility.
- Emergency: Call/message manager immediately; apply in Workday within 24 hours.

SECTION 3: ATTENDANCE RULES & WORK HOURS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

STANDARD WORK HOURS
---------------------
- 40 hours per week; 5-day work week.
- Core collaboration hours: 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM (local time).
- Flexible scheduling strongly encouraged; results over hours philosophy.

HYBRID WORK MODEL
------------------
- Google's official policy (post-2022): 3 days in office per week (Tue, Wed, Thu preferred).
- Fully remote roles: Available for certain positions with VP-level approval.
- Flexible work arrangements for caregivers and special circumstances.
- Office presence tracked via badge swipes (not punitive, used for space planning).
- "20% Time": Googlers are encouraged to spend up to 20% of their time on innovative
  side projects (informal policy; varies by team and project phase).

ATTENDANCE TRACKING
--------------------
- Office: Badge-in/badge-out for security and space utilization.
- Remote: No rigid clock-in; OKR-based output tracking.
- Google Meet attendance for required sync meetings may be tracked by team.

OVERTIME
---------
- Salaried (exempt) employees: No overtime pay; flexibility in lieu.
- Non-exempt employees: Overtime per local labor law.

ABSENTEEISM
------------
- Notify manager for all unexpected absences.
- 3 consecutive unexplained absences: HR follow-up.
- Chronic absenteeism addressed through performance counseling framework.

SECTION 4: BENEFITS & COMPENSATION OVERVIEW
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

HEALTH & WELLNESS (INDIA)
--------------------------
- Mediclaim: Employee + spouse + children + parents. Sum insured: INR 5 Lakhs
  (extendable up to INR 50 Lakhs via additional coverage).
- Group term life insurance: 5x annual CTC.
- Group personal accident: 3x annual CTC.
- Dental and vision coverage.
- Mental health support: Free therapy sessions via EAP; on-campus counselors.
- Google Wellness Reimbursement: Up to INR 20,000/year for gym, fitness tracker, yoga, etc.
- Annual health screening: Fully covered.
- On-site medical clinic/nurse at major campuses.
- "gFit" wellness challenge program with rewards.

FINANCIAL BENEFITS
-------------------
- PF: 12% employee + employer (India).
- Gratuity: Statutory.
- Annual Performance Bonus: Tied to individual rating and company performance.
- Google Stock Units (GSUs/RSUs): Granted to all full-time employees.
  Vesting: 4-year schedule; annual refreshers for top performers.
- Employee Referral Program: Competitive bonus for successful hires.

FAMOUS GOOGLE PERKS (SELECT INDIA-RELEVANT ONES)
--------------------------------------------------
- Free gourmet meals and snacks at office cafeteria ("Googleplex Dining" concept).
- Micro-kitchens on every floor with healthy snacks, beverages, and coffee.
- On-campus gyms, game rooms, massage chairs, and nap pods (at major campuses).
- Laundry and dry-cleaning services on campus.
- Google Hardware purchase discount (Pixel, Nest, Chromebook, etc.).
- Google Play credit and YouTube Premium subscription.
- On-site haircuts, car service, and other concierge services (select large campuses).

LEARNING & DEVELOPMENT
------------------------
- Google Career Certificates: Access to all Google-offered certificate programs.
- Google Cloud, AI/ML, Android, and data engineering training fully funded.
- External Education Reimbursement: Up to USD 12,000/year (US); India equivalent.
- Google's internal courses: "Grow with Google" platform.
- Tech Talk series: Weekly knowledge-sharing sessions by Googlers and external experts.
- 20% Time: Innovation projects encouraged (informal; varies by team).
- G2G (Googler-to-Googler) teaching program: Peer-to-peer workshops on any topic.

SECTION 5: HR PROCESSES & POLICIES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT (GRAD: Googler Reviews and Development)
----------------------------------------------------------------
- OKR-based (Objectives and Key Results) performance model — quarterly reviews.
- Annual performance review: "GRAD" cycle.
- Rating bands: Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations, Needs Improvement, etc.
- 360° feedback from peers, reports, and cross-functional partners.
- Compensation review tied to GRAD outcomes (adjustments, bonuses, equity refreshes).
- Manager excellence rated via "Upward Feedback Survey" (annual).

PROBATION POLICY
-----------------
- India: 6-month probation period.
- Performance and culture fit assessed.
- Confirmation via Workday; extension possible with documented feedback.

CODE OF CONDUCT
----------------
- Google Code of Conduct: "Do the right thing."
- Annual certification mandatory for all Googlers.
- Covers: Conflicts of interest, insider trading, data privacy, harassment, retaliation.
- Google's Ethics & Compliance Hotline: Anonymous, available 24/7.
- Non-retaliation guaranteed for good-faith reports.
- AI Principles: Google's AI ethics guide governs all AI-related work.

SEPARATION & EXIT
------------------
- India notice period: 60–90 days (varies by level and employment contract).
- Resignation via Workday.
- Exit interview: Voluntary but encouraged.
- F&F settlement within 45–60 days.
- Return all Google equipment, badges, and parking passes on last day.

INCLUSION, DIVERSITY & EQUITY
-------------------------------
- Google has ERGs (Employee Resource Groups) for 50+ global communities.
  Examples: Women@Google, Black Googler Network, Gayglers, Googlers with Disabilities.
- India-specific communities for cultural celebrations and advocacy.
- DEI training: Unconscious Bias, Inclusive Leadership, and Allyship courses mandatory.
- Accessibility programs for employees with disabilities.

GRIEVANCE & ETHICS
-------------------
- "Open Door" policy: Any Googler can walk up to any leader.
- HR Business Partner (People Partner) for formal concerns.
- POSH Policy: Dedicated ICC at every India location.
- Ethics Hotline for anonymous reporting.
- Resolution: 30 working days.

INTERNAL MOBILITY
------------------
- Google strongly encourages internal mobility after 12 months in current role.
- Internal Transfer System available on HR portal.
- "Google Careers" internal job board: Post for internal roles globally.
- International transfers supported via Global Mobility team.
