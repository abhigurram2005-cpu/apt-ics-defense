SECTION 1: EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING STEPS
----------------------------

Step 1: Pre-Joining Formalities
- Accept the offer letter via the iBegin portal (TCS's digital onboarding platform).
- Upload mandatory documents: graduation/post-graduation certificates, mark sheets, government-issued ID proof (Aadhaar/Passport/Voter ID), PAN card, address proof, and previous employment documents.
- Complete online pre-joining assessments if applicable (for freshers via Campus Commune / National Qualifier Test).
- Background verification (BGV) initiated through TCS's empaneled third-party agency.

Step 2: ILP – Initial Learning Program (Fresher-specific)
- Freshers join the Initial Learning Program (ILP) held at TCS training centers (Thiruvananthapuram, Pune, Chennai, Hyderabad, etc.).
- Duration: 3–6 months of residential/classroom training.
- Topics: Programming fundamentals, communication skills, Agile, DevOps, domain knowledge.
- Assessment at the end of ILP; allocation to projects based on performance and business requirement.

Step 3: Day 1 – Joining Day (Lateral Hires)
- Report to the designated TCS office with original documents for verification.
- Employee ID card issued; system access and email account (@tcs.com) activated.
- IT asset allocation: laptop, access tokens, VPN credentials.
- Assigned to a buddy/HR SPOC for initial guidance.

Step 4: Orientation Program
- Attend the TCS Induction program covering company history, culture, and values.
- TCS Core Values: Integrity, Responsibility, Excellence, Pioneering, Unity, Respect for the Individual.
- Introduction to internal portals: Ultimatix (employee self-service), iEvolve (learning), GEMS (goal management).
- Mandatory compliance and security training within first 2 weeks.

Step 5: Project Allocation
- Allocation done by the Resource Management Group (RMG) based on skills and project requirements.
- Manager introduction and project-specific onboarding follows allocation.

Step 6: Probation & Confirmation
- Probation period: 6 months for freshers; case-by-case for laterals.
- Confirmation based on performance review at the end of probation.

SECTION 2: HR POLICIES
----------------------------

2.1 Code of Conduct
- All employees must adhere to TCS's Code of Conduct and Ethics Policy.
- Annual acknowledgment of the Code of Conduct is mandatory.
- Zero tolerance for fraud, bribery, and corruption.
- Report concerns via the TCS Ethics Helpline or directly to the Ethics & Compliance team.

2.2 Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) Policy
- TCS follows the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace Act, 2013 (India).
- Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) is constituted at every office location.
- Complaints must be submitted in writing within 3 months of the incident.
- Strict confidentiality maintained during the investigation process.

2.3 Intellectual Property (IP) Policy
- All work products developed during employment are the IP of TCS.
- Employees must not share proprietary code, client data, or project information outside the organization.
- External publications/patents require prior approval from the IP team.

2.4 Acceptable Use of Technology Policy
- TCS systems and networks are to be used for official purposes only.
- Unauthorized software installation is prohibited.
- Personal use of TCS internet is restricted; monitoring may occur.

2.5 Conflict of Interest Policy
- Employees must disclose any personal or financial interest that conflicts with TCS's interests.
- Moonlighting (working for a competitor during employment) is strictly prohibited.
- Disclosure form must be submitted annually.

SECTION 3: LEAVE POLICY
----------------------------

Privilege Leave (PL) / Earned Leave:
- 21 days per year for employees in India.
- Accrued at 1.75 days per month.
- Maximum carry-forward: 90 days (India). Encashment available at separation.

Sick Leave (SL):
- 15 days per year (India).
- Medical certificate required for absence beyond 2 days.
- Not encashable; partial carry-forward permitted in some locations.

Casual Leave (CL):
- 8 days per year. Cannot be combined with PL or carried forward.

Maternity Leave:
- 26 weeks fully paid for female employees in India (first two children).
- 12 weeks for third child onwards.
- Work from home and part-time return-to-work options available post maternity leave.

Paternity Leave:
- 5 days paid leave within 30 days of the child's birth.

Special Caregiver Leave:
- Up to 5 days for care of a critically ill immediate family member (with documentation).

Bereavement Leave:
- 5 days for immediate family (spouse, children, parents, siblings).

Public Holidays:
- 10 national/festival holidays per year (varies by location/state).
- Employees can opt for optional holidays (OPH) as per regional lists.

Leave Application Process:
- Apply via Ultimatix HR portal under the "Leave" module.
- Planned leave requires minimum 3 working days advance notice.
- Leave cancellation must be done via the portal before the leave start date.

SECTION 4: ATTENDANCE POLICY
----------------------------

Working Hours:
- Standard: 9 hours/day (including 30–45 min break), 5 days a week.
- Core hours: 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM.
- Flexible window: 8:00 AM – 10:00 AM (start time).
- Weekly expected hours: 45 hours.

Attendance Tracking:
- Biometric swipe system at office premises.
- Remote attendance tracked via VPN login on Ultimatix.
- Half-day absence if employee works fewer than 5 hours.

Work From Home Policy:
- Hybrid work model: typically 3 days in office, 2 days from home (project-dependent).
- WFH approval via Ultimatix by the reporting manager.
- Employees must maintain full availability via Teams/email during working hours.

Late Arrivals:
- Consistent late arrivals (after 10:30 AM) without approval flagged by HR.
- 3 or more late arrivals in a month may result in half-day leave deduction.

Comp-Off:
- Earned when working on public holidays or weekends.
- Must be applied and availed within 60 days of earning.

Unauthorized Absence:
- 3+ consecutive absent days without leave approval triggers disciplinary review.
- Loss of pay applied for unauthorized absences.

SECTION 5: BENEFITS & COMPENSATION
----------------------------

Health & Insurance:
- Group Mediclaim: Up to INR 5,00,000/year per family (employee + spouse + dependent children).
- Top-up health insurance available at employee's cost.
- Group Term Life Insurance: 3x annual CTC coverage.
- Personal Accident Insurance: 4x annual CTC.

Retirement Benefits:
- Provident Fund (PF): 12% of basic salary contributed by employer (India).
- Gratuity: As per Payment of Gratuity Act (eligible after 5 years of service).
- Superannuation Fund: Employer contributes 15% of basic salary for eligible employees.

Learning & Development:
- iEvolve (TCS Learning Platform): Access to thousands of courses, technical and soft skills.
- TCS Fresco Play: Self-paced online learning with internal and external certifications.
- Higher Education Assistance: Sponsorship for approved MBA/Master's programs.
- Certification reimbursement for approved external certifications.

Allowances (India):
- House Rent Allowance (HRA): Based on city category and basic salary.
- Leave Travel Allowance (LTA): 1 month's basic salary per year (claimable for actual travel).
- Special Allowance and Performance Bonus as per grade.

Employee Wellness:
- Employee Assistance Program (EAP): Confidential counseling services.
- TCS Cares: Employee welfare program covering medical, personal, and social crises.
- Fitness reimbursement available at select locations.
- Annual health check-up for employees.

Rewards & Recognition:
- Star of the Month, Best Team Awards.
- On-the-spot recognition through Gems on Ultimatix.
- Long Service Awards (5 years, 10 years, 15 years, 20 years, 25 years milestones).

----------------------------
SECTION 6: EXIT / SEPARATION POLICY
----------------------------

- Notice Period: 60–90 days based on grade and employment contract.
- Resignation submitted via Ultimatix HR portal.
- Exit interview conducted by HR SPOC or HR Business Partner.
- Knowledge transfer and handover documentation is mandatory.
- Assets (laptop, ID card, access cards) to be returned on last working day.
- Full & Final settlement processed within 45 days post separation.
- Experience letter and relieving letter issued post completion of exit process.
