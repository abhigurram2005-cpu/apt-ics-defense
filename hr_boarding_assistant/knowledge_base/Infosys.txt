SECTION 1: EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING STEPS
----------------------------

Step 1: Pre-Joining Formalities
- Accept offer letter and complete pre-joining formalities via InfyMe app or HR portal.
- Upload required documents: degree certificates, mark sheets, PAN card, Aadhaar, address proof, previous employment documents (offer letter + relieving letter), bank account details.
- Background verification initiated (education, employment, criminal record check).
- Receive joining date confirmation, reporting location, and reporting manager details from HR.

Step 2: Infosys Global Education Center (GEC) – Freshers
- Freshers attend the world-renowned Foundation Program at Infosys Mysuru campus (largest corporate university in the world).
- Duration: 4–5 months of intensive training.
- Curriculum: Programming in Java/.NET/Python, domain fundamentals, communication skills, Agile methodologies, leadership workshops.
- Continuous assessment; employees must pass to proceed to project allocation.
- Accommodation provided on-campus at Mysuru during training.

Step 3: Day 1 – Joining Day (Lateral Hires)
- Report to the designated Infosys office with original documents.
- Employee ID and Infosys email (@infosys.com) activated.
- IT assets provisioned: laptop, VPN credentials, system access.
- Buddy program: each new employee assigned an Infosys buddy for first 90 days.

Step 4: Induction Program
- Attend mandatory Infosys Induction covering:
  - Company vision, mission, and values (C-LIFE: Client Value, Leadership by Example, Integrity and Transparency, Fairness, Excellence).
  - Introduction to Sparsh (HR portal), InfyMe app, Lex (learning platform).
  - Compliance training, information security, and code of conduct sessions.

Step 5: Project Allocation
- Allocation by Resource Management team based on GEC performance (freshers) or skills (laterals).
- Joining project team and reporting manager introduction.
- Access to project tools, client systems (if applicable), and collaboration platforms.

Step 6: Probation & Confirmation
- Probation: 6 months from date of joining.
- Mid-probation check-in at 3 months; final review at 6 months.
- Confirmation letter issued post successful review.

SECTION 2: HR POLICIES
----------------------------

2.1 Infosys Code of Conduct
- Employees must read, understand, and sign the Code of Conduct at joining.
- Principles of Honesty, Accountability, and Professionalism must be maintained at all times.
- Annual re-acknowledgment via the Sparsh portal.
- Whistleblower mechanism available via the Ethics Reporting Portal.

2.2 Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH)
- Zero-tolerance policy for sexual harassment at the workplace.
- ICC (Internal Complaints Committee) at all Infosys locations.
- POSH awareness training mandatory for all employees within first month.
- Complaints to be submitted within 90 days of the incident.

2.3 Information Security Policy
- Employees must not access, copy, or transmit client or company data to unauthorized parties.
- Clean desk policy enforced in all offices.
- Mandatory annual information security awareness training and certification.
- Use of personal USB drives, screen capture tools, and unauthorized cloud storage is prohibited.

2.4 Non-Compete & Non-Solicitation Policy
- Employees agree to non-compete and non-solicitation clauses during the notice period.
- Joining a direct competitor within 6 months of leaving Infosys requires review and approval.

2.5 Dress Code Policy
- Smart casual or business casual in all Infosys offices.
- Formal attire required for client visits and executive meetings.
- Neat and professional appearance is mandatory at all times.

SECTION 3: LEAVE POLICY
----------------------------

Earned/Privilege Leave (EL/PL):
- 24 days per year for India-based employees.
- Accrued at 2 days per month.
- Maximum carry-forward: 60 days (India). Excess can be encashed annually.

Sick Leave (SL):
- 12 days per year. 
- Self-certification acceptable for up to 3 days; medical certificate required for longer absences.
- Cannot be carried forward or encashed.

Casual Leave (CL):
- 6 days per year. Non-encashable; cannot be carried forward.

Maternity Leave:
- 26 weeks fully paid (in compliance with Maternity Benefit Act, 2017 – India).
- Extended leave (up to 1 year total including EL) may be negotiated with HR.
- Flexi-work options (part-time or WFH) available for 6 months post return.

Paternity Leave:
- 7 days paid leave within 3 months of child's birth or adoption.

Adoption Leave:
- 12 weeks for the primary adoptive parent; 5 days for secondary.

Bereavement Leave:
- 5 days for immediate family members.
- 2 days for extended family.

Election Duty Leave:
- Paid leave for employees serving as election officials (with appointment letter).

Leave Application Process:
- Apply via Sparsh portal under "Leave Management."
- Minimum 5 working days advance notice for planned EL.
- Sick and emergency leave can be applied same day with notification to manager.

SECTION 4: ATTENDANCE POLICY
----------------------------

Working Hours:
- Standard: 8.5–9 hours/day (including 30-minute break), 5 days/week (Monday–Friday).
- Core hours: 10:00 AM – 4:30 PM (employees must be available).
- Flexible start: 8:00 AM – 10:00 AM.

Attendance Tracking:
- Badge swipe at office entry/exit points.
- VPN login tracked for remote employees.
- Attendance data available on Sparsh portal.

Work From Home:
- Hybrid model in effect: typically 3 days in office/week.
- WFH approval required from manager via Sparsh.
- Employees on WFH must be available on Teams/email during all core hours.

Late Arrivals:
- Two consecutive late arrivals (post 10:30 AM) trigger manager alert.
- Habitual lateness (5+ instances/month) may result in leave deduction and HR counseling.

Comp-Off:
- Issued for work done on declared holidays or weekends.
- Must be availed within 90 days of accrual.

Unauthorized Absence:
- More than 2 consecutive absent days without leave approval treated as LOP (Loss of Pay).
- 5 consecutive absent days may trigger job abandonment review.

SECTION 5: BENEFITS & COMPENSATION
----------------------------

Health & Insurance:
- Group Health Insurance (GHI): INR 4,00,000–8,00,000/year (employee + family).
- Group Term Life Insurance: 3x CTC.
- Group Personal Accident Cover: 4x CTC.
- Infosys provides hospitalization cashless facility at empaneled hospitals.

Retirement:
- Provident Fund (PF): 12% employer contribution.
- Gratuity: As per Gratuity Act (after 5 years of service).
- Superannuation: 15% of basic salary (for eligible grade levels).

Learning & Development:
- Lex (Learning Experience Platform): Personalized learning paths with 200,000+ learning assets.
- InfyTQ for engineering upskilling.
- Certification reimbursement: Up to INR 20,000 per year.
- Infosys Leadership Institute (ILI) programs for senior employees.
- External conference and academic sponsorship available for approved candidates.

Allowances (India):
- HRA as per grade and city classification.
- LTA: Claimable twice in a 4-year block.
- Books & Periodicals allowance: INR 1,250/month.
- Telephone/Mobile reimbursement: Up to INR 1,500/month (eligible grades).

Wellness Programs:
- Infosys HALE (Health Assessment & Lifestyle Enrichment): On-campus medical centers, gyms, recreational facilities (at major campuses).
- Employee Assistance Program (EAP): Confidential counseling services.
- Annual preventive health check-up for employees and spouses.
- Mental health and resilience workshops offered periodically.

Rewards & Recognition:
- SPOT Awards (on-the-spot recognition by managers).
- Insta Award and WOW Awards for outstanding contributions.
- Long Service Awards at 5, 10, 15, 20, 25-year milestones.
- Infosys Prize (for exceptional intellectual achievement).

----------------------------
SECTION 6: EXIT / SEPARATION POLICY
----------------------------

- Notice Period: 60–90 days based on grade (as per appointment letter).
- Resignation submitted via Sparsh portal.
- Exit interview with HR before last working day.
- Knowledge transfer document and handover checklist mandatory.
- Asset return (laptop, access card, library books) on last working day.
- Full & Final settlement within 45 days post last working day.
- Relieving and experience letters issued within 30 days post exit process completion.
