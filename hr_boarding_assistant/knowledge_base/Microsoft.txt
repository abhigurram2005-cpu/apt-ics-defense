SECTION 1: EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING STEPS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

STEP 1 – PRE-JOINING (Microsoft Careers & HireRight)
------------------------------------------------------
- Accept offer letter through the Microsoft Offer Management Portal.
- Complete I-9 / equivalent employment eligibility (USA); country-specific compliance forms elsewhere.
- Upload documents: government-issued ID, academic certificates, previous employment letters.
- Background check conducted by HireRight (or authorized regional partner).
- Receive "Welcome to Microsoft" email with Day 1 instructions, login credentials, and resources.
- Pre-access setup: Microsoft Account provisioned 48–72 hours before Day 1.
- Access Microsoft's internal New Hire Portal: "Microsoft New Employee Hub."
- Download Microsoft Authenticator for MFA setup.

STEP 2 – DAY 1: WELCOME & FOUNDATIONS
---------------------------------------
- Attend the "Day 1 Experience" orientation — available on-site or virtually via Microsoft Teams.
- Receive equipment: Surface device, Microsoft 365 license, and access badge.
- Microsoft employee ID provisioned in the Active Directory system.
- Attend the "Welcome to Microsoft" session with HR and business leadership.
- Introduction to Microsoft's mission: "Empower every person and organization on the planet to achieve more."
- Growth Mindset culture overview (Satya Nadella's "Growth Mindset" framework).
- Set up Microsoft 365 apps: Teams, Outlook, SharePoint, and Azure AD.

STEP 3 – WEEK 1: COMPLIANCE & ORIENTATION
--------------------------------------------
- Complete mandatory compliance training in Microsoft Learn / Microsoft Viva Learning:
  - Standards of Business Conduct (SBC) training
  - Microsoft Privacy and Security Awareness
  - Workplace Harassment & Discrimination Prevention
  - Export Compliance (if applicable to role)
- Connect with your assigned "Onboarding Buddy."
- Attend team introductions and 1:1 with direct manager.
- Review role expectations, team wiki, and project documentation.

STEP 4 – FIRST 30 DAYS
------------------------
- Complete benefits enrollment within 30 days (medical, dental, vision, 401k/DCPF India equivalent).
- Submit tax declaration and payroll details via HR Self-Service (Success@Work portal — India).
- Attend "Microsoft Fundamentals" learning path on Microsoft Learn.
- Join relevant internal communities on Viva Engage (formerly Yammer).
- Explore Microsoft's Employee Resource Groups (ERGs).
- Set 30-60-90 day goals with manager via Microsoft Viva Goals.

STEP 5 – 90-DAY MILESTONE
---------------------------
- Manager-led review of progress, culture fit, and goals.
- Finalize development plan.
- Probation review (for applicable regions including India).
- Enroll in Microsoft Aspire program (specifically for new graduates — 2-year career development program).

SECTION 2: LEAVE POLICY (INDIA)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

ANNUAL / PRIVILEGE LEAVE
--------------------------
- Entitlement: 24 days per year.
- Accrued at 2 days per month.
- Carry forward: Up to 30 days.
- Minimum 5 working days advance notice for planned leaves.
- Encashable at separation.

SICK LEAVE
-----------
- Entitlement: 12 days per year.
- Non-encashable; lapses at year-end.
- Medical certificate required for 3+ consecutive sick days.

CASUAL LEAVE
-------------
- 10 days per year.
- No carry forward.

MATERNITY LEAVE
----------------
- 26 weeks for first and second child.
- 12 weeks for third child onwards.
- Microsoft enhanced maternity benefit: Full pay for entire statutory period.
- Phased return-to-work option available (reduced hours for first 4 weeks on return).

PATERNITY LEAVE
----------------
- Microsoft provides 12 weeks paid paternity leave — significantly above statutory norms.
- Can be split and taken within 12 months of birth/adoption.

PRIMARY & SECONDARY CAREGIVER LEAVE
--------------------------------------
- Primary caregiver: Up to 20 weeks paid leave (inclusive of maternity/paternity).
- Secondary caregiver: Up to 4 weeks paid leave.
- Supports adoption, foster care, and surrogacy.

BEREAVEMENT LEAVE
------------------
- Immediate family: Up to 5 days.
- Extended family: Up to 3 days.

MARRIAGE LEAVE
---------------
- 5 working days for own wedding (once during employment).

EMERGENCY LEAVE
----------------
- Up to 3 days for unexpected personal/family emergencies (at manager discretion).

VOLUNTEERING LEAVE
-------------------
- Microsoft provides 3 days of paid volunteer leave per year.
- Linked to Microsoft Employee Giving Program.

LEAVE APPLICATION PROCESS
---------------------------
- Apply via Success@Work portal (India) or Microsoft's global HR system.
- Manager approval required for all planned leaves.
- Emergency leaves: notify manager immediately; formal application within 24 hours.

SECTION 3: ATTENDANCE RULES & WORK HOURS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

STANDARD WORK HOURS
---------------------
- 40 hours per week; Monday to Friday.
- Core collaboration hours: 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM (local time).
- Flexible start and end times with manager alignment.
- Microsoft operates a "Hybrid Workplace" model globally.

FLEXIBLE WORK ARRANGEMENTS
----------------------------
- Microsoft supports flexible hours and work locations.
- Each employee's "Flexibility Plan" is agreed upon with their manager.
- Remote work: Up to 50% of working time permitted without formal approval (varies by team).
- Working from another country: Requires advance HR and legal approval.
- Core in-office expectations are team/business-unit specific.

ATTENDANCE MONITORING
----------------------
- Office attendance tracked via building access badge.
- Remote employees: No rigid check-in; performance and output-focused culture.
- Manager discretion on team scheduling.

OVERTIME
---------
- Exempt (salaried) employees: No overtime pay; recognized via comp-off or flexible time.
- Non-exempt employees: Overtime paid per local labor law.

ABSENTEEISM
------------
- All absences must be reported via Success@Work portal or manager notification.
- 3+ days unplanned absence: HR outreach.
- Chronic absenteeism handled through performance improvement process.

SECTION 4: BENEFITS & COMPENSATION OVERVIEW
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

HEALTH BENEFITS (INDIA)
------------------------
- Mediclaim insurance: Employee + spouse + children + parents (up to INR 5 Lakhs;
  extendable to INR 10 Lakhs via top-up plan).
- Group term life insurance: 5x annual CTC.
- Group personal accident: 3x annual CTC.
- Dental and vision coverage included.
- Mental health: Employee Assistance Program (EAP), free confidential therapy sessions.
- Wellness wallet: INR 15,000/year for gym, wellness apps, or nutrition programs.
- Annual health check-up: Fully paid at partner diagnostic labs.

FINANCIAL BENEFITS
-------------------
- PF: 12% employee + employer match (India).
- Gratuity: Statutory (after 5 years).
- Annual Performance Bonus: Based on individual and company performance.
- Microsoft Stock Awards (RSUs): Granted to most full-time employees.
  Vesting schedule: 4 years with cliff after year 1.
- Employee Stock Purchase Plan (ESPP): Buy Microsoft stock at a 10% discount.
- Referral Bonus: Competitive rewards for successful hires.

LEARNING & DEVELOPMENT
------------------------
- Microsoft Learn: Free access to all Microsoft certifications and learning paths.
- GitHub Copilot, Azure, AI/ML certifications funded by Microsoft.
- LinkedIn Learning: Unlimited access for all employees.
- Education Assistance Program: Up to INR 1,50,000/year for approved degrees/courses.
- Microsoft Aspire Program: 2-year development program for early-in-career hires.
- Internal Hackathons: Garage sessions and innovation labs.

ADDITIONAL PERKS
-----------------
- Microsoft 365 Home subscription for personal use.
- Microsoft Employee Purchase Program: Discounted Surface devices, Xbox, and software.
- Commute subsidy: Transport allowance or company cab service.
- Microsoft Gives: 3 paid volunteer days + donation matching up to USD 15,000/year.
- Diversity & Inclusion focused Employee Resource Groups (ERGs): 40+ groups globally.
- Adoption assistance and fertility support.

SECTION 5: HR PROCESSES & POLICIES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT (Microsoft Connects)
---------------------------------------------
- Continuous performance feedback model: "Connects" — regular 1:1 conversations.
- No forced bell curve; growth-oriented performance culture.
- Annual performance review: Impact assessed on: Own Impact, Leveraged Others' Impact,
  Modeling the Way.
- Compensation adjustments and bonus tied to performance review outcomes.
- Tools: Microsoft Viva Goals, Microsoft Viva Insights, Manager dashboards.

PROBATION POLICY
-----------------
- India: 6-month probation period.
- Confirmation based on performance, behavior, and culture fit.
- Extension possible with documented feedback.

STANDARDS OF BUSINESS CONDUCT (SBC)
--------------------------------------
- Microsoft's SBC covers: Ethics, Anti-corruption, Privacy, Competition Law.
- Annual SBC certification mandatory for all employees.
- MS Ethics & Compliance Hotline: Available 24/7 for anonymous reporting.
- Non-retaliation policy for whistleblowers.
- AI ethics principles embedded in all technology-related work policies.

SEPARATION & EXIT
------------------
- Notice period (India): 60–90 days (varies by band/level).
- Resignation via Success@Work.
- Exit interview with HR Business Partner.
- F&F settlement within 45–60 days.
- Device and access return mandatory on last working day.

GRIEVANCE & INCLUSION
-----------------------
- Microsoft Harassment-Free Workplace policy: Zero tolerance.
- POSH ICC at every India office.
- Global HR Compliance team handles cross-border issues.
- Open Door policy: Employees can escalate to any level of leadership.
- Resolution timeline: 30 working days.

INTERNAL MOBILITY
------------------
- Employees can explore internal roles after 18 months in current role (India & Global).
- Internal Transfer Tool: Available on Microsoft HR intranet.
- Global mobility (relocation to another country) facilitated by Microsoft's Mobility team.
